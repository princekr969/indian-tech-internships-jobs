## What This Role Is About

> *From the JD: "The Digital Platforms & Experiences team is part of the fast-growing Global Marketing function at BlackRock which seeks to scale our platforms, embody our brand and better engage a multi-trillion-dollar audience of investors, advisors and institutions."*

You'll be on BlackRock's **Digital Platforms & Experiences team** inside the **Global Marketing function** — the team that builds the digital experience layer that BlackRock's clients, advisors, and institutions interact with across **web, mobile, and digital assets**. This is a **mid-level "Associate" role** (BlackRock's early-career professional band — typically maps to **~2-4 years of experience**, NOT a fresh-grad SDE-1).

**The work you do:**
- Spearhead **frontend development** — architecting + building projects
- **Leverage AI actively** in daily development + testing (this is now an explicit JD requirement, not a nice-to-have)
- Own features from inception to delivery
- Work through the **entire SDLC** with project managers, technical leads, business owners, analysts
- Participate in **Agile/Scrum**, set coding standards + best practices
- Support existing systems + resolve operational issues globally
- Stay curious, embrace work outside your comfort zone

> **Reality check:** This is **enterprise web platform engineering at a top-tier financial firm** — not a fintech startup. Expect high-bar coding standards, strong review culture, and a focus on **AI-augmented development** (using AI as a daily tool, not as an afterthought).
