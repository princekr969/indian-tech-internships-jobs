## About Synchrony

- NYSE-listed consumer financial services company (NYSE: SYF)
- ~$100B+ in assets, top US private-label credit-card issuer
- India operations: **Hyderabad (Central Hub)** + virtual hubs in Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Pune, Kolkata
- 20,000+ employees globally, 5,000+ in India tech
- Major focus on credit, risk, fraud, technology-led finance

## What This Role Does

- 🛡️ **Lead the ServiceNow IRM platform** within Synchrony
- 🏗️ Own **architectural design** of Integrated Risk Management solutions
- 🔧 Implement **Policy & Compliance, Audit, Risk, Vendor Risk** modules
- 🤝 **Tech Product Owner** for IRM suite — drive roadmap + priorities
- ☁️ Enable **Enterprise Cloud Migration + AI adoption** for risk/compliance
- 👥 Lead small team of ServiceNow developers
- 🔄 Expand to ITSM, ITAM, ITOM modules as bandwidth allows
- 📊 Work with US + India risk stakeholders

## Required Skills & Certifications

- 🎓 **Bachelor's degree** (any field) **+ 6+ years** Service Management tools in technical capacity
- 🪪 **Certified ServiceNow System Administrator** (CSA) **mandatory**
- 🪪 **Certified Application Developer** (CAD) + 1+ year experience **mandatory**
- 🏗️ **6+ years hands-on** ServiceNow Configuration Management
- 🛠️ **Hands-on implementation** of ServiceNow IRM (Policy & Compliance, Vulnerability, Audit, Risk, Vendor Risk)
- 📚 **ITIL V3 / V4 Foundations** certified
- 🏦 **Financial services / credit card / insurance** experience preferred
- 🗣️ Strong stakeholder management (US + India)
