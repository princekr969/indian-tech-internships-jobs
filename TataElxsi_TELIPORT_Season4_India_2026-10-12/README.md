## What This Programme Is

> *From HireToday: "Tata Elxsi TELIPORT Season 4 is a technology and innovation competition for engineering students focused on AI-first design and product engineering. Teams select a problem statement, develop an innovative solution and present it through multiple submission rounds before the Grand Finale."*

TELIPORT is Tata Elxsi's flagship **national-level case challenge** that doubles as a **PPO (Pre-Placement Offer) funnel**. The 2024 season (Season 3) had 17,241 teams, with Top 6 teams becoming national finalists. **Season 4** is the 4th edition in 2026-27.

**What you get out of it (best case):**
- **National Finalist** → PPO at Tata Elxsi (₹10 LPA CTC possible)
- **Round 2 Selected** → PPO Interview opportunity
- All Round 2+ participants → direct visibility with Tata Elxsi hiring team

> **Tip:** Past Season 3 winners landed at Tata Elxsi in embedded systems, low-level C/Python, and product engineering roles (per LinkedIn profiles of Season 3 finalists).

### Round 1 — Idea Submission
- Pick a problem statement (Tata Elxsi publishes problem themes)
- Submit your AI-first / product engineering solution concept
- Teams selected in Round 1 advance to Round 2

### Round 2 — Prototype / Detailed Submission
- Build a working prototype / deeper solution
- Get reviewed by Tata Elxsi experts
- **Top teams selected in Round 2 get PPO Interview opportunity** (per 9curry)

### Round 3 / Grand Finale — National Finals
- National finalists present to Tata Elxsi leadership
- **National finalists receive Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs)**

## How to Apply — Step by Step

1. Open [gradpartners.in/e/Tata-Elxsi-TELIPORT-Season-4-347](https://gradpartners.in/e/Tata-Elxsi-TELIPORT-Season-4-347)
2. Sign up / log in to GradPartners
3. Fill out profile + college details + branch + batch year
4. Pick the problem statement(s) that align with your skills
5. Form a team (if team-based) — all members from same college
6. Submit your Round 1 idea before **Oct 21-23, 2026** (verify exact deadline on apply form)
7. Track progress at [gradpartners.in](https://gradpartners.in/opportunities?type=opportunity)
8. If shortlisted, prepare for Round 2 prototype submission
9. Grand Finale: March 30, 2027

### Application Tips (from past Season 3 finalists)

- **Pick a problem statement where you have unique domain knowledge** — judges see hundreds of generic AI submissions
- **Build a working prototype** — not just slides; demos > decks
- **Show real impact** — quantify the problem (e.g., "this affects 10M users daily", "current solutions are 40% inaccurate")
- **Use Tata Elxsi's industry verticals** (automotive, healthcare, media) — align with their business
- **Differentiate on design** — Tata Elxsi is a design-led company; UX matters
