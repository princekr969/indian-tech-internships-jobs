## About Synchrony

- NYSE-listed consumer financial services company (NYSE: SYF)
- ~$100B+ in assets, top US private-label credit-card issuer
- India operations: **Hyderabad (Central Hub)** + virtual hubs in Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Pune, Kolkata
- 20,000+ employees globally, 5,000+ in India tech
- Analytics team is a **strategic partner** to the entire Synchrony business

## What This Role Does

- 📈 **Lead data-driven decisions** across Synchrony's business units
- 🛠️ **Build analytics solutions** to optimize product + platform performance
- 🤝 Partner with **marketing, risk, fraud, ops, credit** teams
- 📊 **Develop metrics, dashboards, models** that drive profitable growth
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Lead a small team of analysts + partner with US stakeholders
- 🔁 Work in **agile / cross-functional pods** (Office of Agile setup)
- ☁️ Use tools: SAS, Python, SQL, Tableau, Power BI

## Tech & Tools

- **Languages:** SQL · SAS · Python · R
- **Visualization:** Tableau · Power BI · Superset
- **Data:** ETL/ELT pipelines, data warehousing, Snowflake
- **ML / AI:** Statistical modeling, AI applications, GenAI familiarity a plus
- **Bonus:** Marketing analytics, customer analytics, credit-risk analytics
- **Work timings:** US Eastern hours overlap (2 PM – 11 PM IST typical)

## Requirements

- 🎓 **Master's preferred** in Statistics, Economics, Mathematics, or quantitative field
  - Or **Bachelor's + 3-5 years** analytics experience
  - Or **5-7 years** analytics without degree
- 📊 Strong in **SQL, SAS, Python** (at least 2 of 3)
- 📈 **3+ years SAS + SQL experience** + 1+ year Python (preferred)
- 📉 **2+ years** of data visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI, etc.)
- 🤖 Healthy understanding of **AI applications** in analytics
- 🗣️ Excellent **stakeholder communication** (US + India)
- 💳 BFSI / financial services / credit / marketing analytics experience a plus
