## What This Role Is About

> *From the JD: "Meta is seeking an ASIC Design Engineer Intern to join our Infrastructure organization. Our servers and data centers are the foundation upon which our rapidly scaling infrastructure efficiently operates."*

You'll be an integral member of an **ASIC (Application-Specific Integrated Circuit) team** at Meta's Infrastructure organization — the team that designs custom silicon chips for Meta's data centers. You'll work on building **accelerators for some of Meta's top workloads** (think: AI/ML training, inference, video transcoding, ranking systems) that enable data centers to scale efficiently and sustainably.

**The team is building "Green" data center accelerators** — purpose-built chips for efficiency, performance, and scale. Meta has been a major player in custom silicon since the MTIA (Meta Training and Inference Accelerator) project.

**Internship is 12-16 weeks long.** US-based candidates typically work in summer (May-Aug), India-based candidates can be flexible based on academic calendar.

---

## What You'll Do

- Participate in **Micro-architecture, Design, and Verification reviews** and provide feedback
- **Design and develop RTL or HLS code** for IPs (Intellectual Property blocks)
- **Analyze designs and enhance PPA** (Power, Performance, Area) — the holy trinity of chip design
- **Support and develop Verification Infrastructure**, analyze and improve Verification Coverage
- Support **Simulation accelerators** and **post-Silicon validation**

## Selection Process (Inferred Meta Pattern)

```
Resume + Application
     ↓
Recruiter Phone/Video Screen (45-60 min)
  - Background, motivation, fit
  - Verify HDL + computer architecture knowledge
  - Eligibility + work authorization check
     ↓
Technical Phone/Video Interview (60-90 min)
  - Computer architecture deep-dive (cache coherency, pipelining, memory hierarchy)
  - HDL coding round (Verilog/SV)
  - RTL design question (state machine, FIFO, ALU design)
     ↓
Onsite / Virtual Loop (3-4 rounds)
  - Round 1: RTL design coding + verification
  - Round 2: Computer architecture (cache, pipeline, branch prediction, TLB, virtual memory)
  - Round 3: SystemVerilog / UVM-based verification discussion
  - Round 4: Behavioral / culture fit
     ↓
Offer (typically within 1-2 weeks after final round)
```
