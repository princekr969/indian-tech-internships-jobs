## 🎯 Strategy to Win

### Round 1 — LinkedIn Zip Puzzle Tips

- 🧠 **Practice daily** — speed matters, average solving time determines rank
- ⏰ **Solve at 12:30 PM IST** — new puzzle drops daily; early solves are faster
- 🔁 **Be consistent** — 10+ solves minimum, but 30+ improves your average
- 📊 **Track your time** — aim for Top 50 average
- 🧩 **Brush logic, deduction, sliding tiles, sudoku-like puzzles** — Zip is similar

### Round 2 — LinkedIn Posts Tips

- 🎯 **Stick to the 5 official prompts** exactly
- 🪶 **Tell a career story** — strongest content comes from real experience
- 🤝 **Tag #MyLinkedInEra #BeUnstoppable** in every post
- 📈 **Engagement matters** — encourage friends to like/comment/repost
- 🎨 **Mix formats** — text posts, carousels, images, videos
- 📚 **Solve a problem for the reader** — actionable advice works best
