## Selection Process (Full Pipeline)

> ⚠️ Juspay has a **long, elimination-heavy** process — expect **~4 weeks total**.

| Stage | Format | Duration | What It Tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Resume Shortlist** | LinkedIn / Juspay careers portal | — | Projects + DSA foundation |
| **2. Online Coding Test (Round 1)** | DSA — 2-3 problems | 90 min | Graphs, trees, sliding window, recursion |
| **3. Online Assessment (Round 2)** | CS fundamentals MCQs + DSA | 60 min | OS, DBMS, Networking, OOP, aptitude |
| **4. Hackathon Part A** | HackerEye hackathon | 3 hours | **"Tree of Space" problem** (lock / unlock / upgrade tree operations) |
| **5. Elimination Round** | Google Meet live coding | 1 hour | 2 DSA problems + intro + projects discussion |
| **6. Hackathon Part B** | Extended hackathon | 3+ hours | Multi-threaded Tree-of-Space (race conditions, mutex, semaphore) |
| **7. System Round** | Tech interview | 40-60 min | OS · DBMS · Networking · OOP · Concurrency · System Design · Resume · DSA · Logical/probability |
| **8. Final Interview** | HR / culture fit | 30-45 min | Motivation, payments passion, joining details |

### 🎯 Critical Prep — "Tree of Space" Problem

Juspay's hackathon famously uses the **"Tree of Space"** problem repeatedly. You should:

- 📚 Study tree operations: `lock(node, user)`, `unlock(node, user)`, `upgrade(node, user)`
- 🧵 For Part B: handle **race conditions**, **thread safety**, **mutex / semaphore concepts**
- 💻 Practice on HackerEarth / Codeforces tree-dominant problems
- ⚡ Be ready to **optimize time + space** under interviewer pressure

### Juspay-Specific Interview Flavor

- 💰 **Payments-domain thinking:** failed transactions, duplicate callbacks, retries, timeouts, reconciliation mismatches, idempotency
- 🏗️ **Concurrency deep-dive:** HashMap internals, TCP vs UDP, thread-safety, database indexing
- 🎯 **System design focus:** payment flow architecture, retry logic, observability, scale
- 💬 **Why Juspay?** Be ready with: passion for payments, global infrastructure, FP at Microsoft, functional programming interest
