## About American Express CodeStreet

- 🎯 **CodeStreet** is American Express India's flagship campus talent hunt
- 🛠️ Multi-stage: aptitude → technical → business case → finale
- 🏆 Top teams / finalists get **Pre-Placement Interviews (PPIs)** at AmEx
- 📈 Best path to **AmEx SWE-I Bengaluru** (₹34 LPA fixed — see `AmericanExpress_SWEI_Bengaluru_2026-10-09/` README)
- 🧪 Focused on **Software + FinTech + AI** problem statements
- 🏆 Top-performing interns can get **performance-based stipend up to ₹10,000** during evaluation internship

## Why CodeStreet

- 🎯 **Direct pathway to AmEx SWE-I ₹34 LPA**
- 🏆 **Less competitive** than direct AmEx applications — code + business case, not just LeetCode
- 📊 **Finale in Chennai** typically (per aggregator data)
- 🧪 **AI-focused** track — current trend
- 📜 **Certificate + recognition** from AmEx

## Eligibility (Most Common)

- 🎓 **Final-year engineering students** (B.E. / B.Tech / M.Tech)
- 📅 **Graduating batch: 2027** (verify per cohort)
- 🏫 **Participating engineering colleges** (AmEx recruits across tier-1 / tier-2 / select colleges)
- 🛠️ Strong **DSA + problem-solving**
- 🧠 Bonus: **finance / payments / AI / ML** exposure

> ⚠️ **College eligibility is selective** — CodeStreet is typically held at **AmEx-recruited colleges** (tier-1 + select tier-2). Check with your placement coordinator.

## Selection Process (Typical Format)

| Stage | Format | What It Tests |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Registration** | College-level (TPO) | — |
| **2. Online Screening** | Coding challenge (HackerRank / HackerEarth) | DSA + aptitude |
| **3. Business Case Round** | PPT + business thinking | Analytical + presentation |
| **4. Technical Interview** | Tech interview (DSA + project) | Engineering depth |
| **5. Final Round (Chennai)** | Onsite finale at AmEx office | Full case + technical |
| **6. PPI for SWE-I ₹34 LPA** | Direct interview slot | Final interview for FTE |
