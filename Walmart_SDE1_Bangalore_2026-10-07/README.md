| Round | Format | What It Tests |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Online Assessment** | CodeSignal / HackerRank, ~90 min, 2-3 medium problems | **DSA filter** — clean solutions + correct complexity |

## Walmart-Specific Interview Flavor

Walmart interviews **emphasize practical engineering judgement over academic depth**:
- 🎯 **Retail-scale reasoning** — design for 100x peak loads
- 🛍️ **Black Friday / Diwali sale** problems (extreme seasonality)
- 🏬 **Inventory sync** across 10,000 stores + e-commerce
- 📦 **Catalog scale** problems (millions of SKUs)
- 🧠 Trade-offs between consistency, availability, partition tolerance

> **Pro tip:** Mention any **retail, e-commerce, supply-chain, or inventory projects** you have built — even personal/academic ones. Walmart loves that context.
