## 🎯 What This Role Is About

**Sony Research India** is one of Sony Group's global research labs (alongside Japan, the United States and Europe), running frontier work in **AI and data analytics**. It exists to create new technology, products and services while sustaining Sony Group's businesses across **electronics, entertainment and financial services** — and to build a **global technology talent bank** of research and engineering talent.

As a **Data Science / AI Intern**, you accelerate Sony's **next generation of predictive algorithms and video analysis architectures**. ## ✅ What You'll Do (Official Responsibilities)

1. **Analyze Massive Datasets** — mine, clean and analyse high-dimensional user-interaction data, identify latent behavioural structures, and transform noisy signals into highly optimized feature spaces.
2. **Translate data annotations** into actionable insights.
