Choose **ONE** challenge to focus on. Your project must:
- ✅ Use AMD infrastructure (DevCloud, Instinct, ROCm, Ryzen AI, Radeon)

- ✅ Use LLMs, computer vision, audio, agents, predictive models — or combo
