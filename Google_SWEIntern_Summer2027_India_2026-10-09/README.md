## 🏢 Where Google Hires From (On-Campus Visits)

Google India recruits from:
- ✅ **All IITs** (especially top 5 — Bombay, Delhi, Madras, Kanpur, Kharagpur)
- ✅ **IIIT Hyderabad** (a major Google feeder)
- ✅ **BITS Pilani** (all campuses)
- ✅ **Top NITs** (limited)
- ⚠️ **IIITM Gwalior:** Stretch — Google does not regularly visit, but students get offers via off-campus + referrals

> **For ABV-IIITM Gwalior:** Google hiring is **mostly off-campus + referral-based** for non-IIT colleges. Strong alumni network + cold emailing are key.

## ✅ Eligibility & Selection

### Eligibility
- 🎓 **2027 Bachelor's or Master's graduate** in CS / related
- 📅 **Strong DSA fundamentals** (acceptance rate estimated 2-5% per community trackers)
- 💻 **1+ production-grade project** at scale
- 🌍 **No prior full-time experience** (internships OK)
- 📊 **Strong academic record** (CGPA not strict cut-off but competitive)

### Selection Process

| Stage | Format | What It Tests |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Resume screen** | ATS + recruiter review | Projects + skills + impact |
| **2. Online Assessment** | HackerRank-style | 2-3 DSA problems (medium-hard) |
| **3. Technical Phone Screen** | 45-60 min (Google Docs) | DSA + coding |
| **4. Virtual Onsite (4-5 rounds)** | 45 min each | DSA + system design + behavioral |
| **5. Hiring Committee** | Internal review | Cross-team calibration |
| **6. Offer** | Via recruiter | — |

**Total timeline:** 6-12 weeks. **Cooldown:** 6-12 months if rejected.
