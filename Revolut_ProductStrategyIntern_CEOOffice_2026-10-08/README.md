## What You'll Do (CEO Office, Strategy)

> *"You will be supporting senior executives and the CEO on the following areas:"*

- 🎯 **Develop a point of view** on specific fintech verticals — define cohesive long-term strategy for company + product departments
- 👁️ **Market intelligence** — track competitive position, product performance globally; feed insights back to strategy + department roadmaps
- 🚀 **Scope and launch new verticals** — act as eyes and ears for new product opportunities
- 📊 **Build business cases** for new products and features that drive Revolut's hyper-growth
- 🌐 **Define initial product proposition + go-to-market** strategies for new expansion markets
- 📈 **Work on 0-1 product strategy** for new verticals

### Day-in-the-Life

- 🔍 Conduct research and analysis on market trends, competition, customer behaviour
- 🧠 Synthesise insights into product roadmap recommendations
- 💼 Present to senior leadership + CEO Office
- 🌍 Collaborate with **Expansion and Territories teams** across global markets

## Eligibility & Requirements

### Must-Haves

- 🎓 **STEM or highly quantitative degree** from a top-tier university
  - Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, Economics, Business Analytics, etc.
- 🏆 **Track record of outstanding achievement** in different areas (academic, professional, extracurricular)
- 💼 **Prior internship experience** in business / product / strategy at:
  - Top-tier **consultancy** (McKinsey / BCG / Bain / etc.)
  - **Investment bank** / **PE / VC**
  - **Fast-growing tech company** (fintech / consumer / SaaS)
- 🧠 **Excellent problem-solving** skills
- ❤️ **Empathy for customers** + customer-centric approach to design

### Good-to-Haves

- 📊 Track record of building successful products (ideally financial) using data + customer feedback + ML
- 💻 Familiarity with fintech products, payments, banking
- 🌍 Multi-language a plus (English required; additional EU/Asian languages a plus)
- 📈 Experience with 0-1 product launches

- 🎯 **CEO Office roles are elite** — be ready for strategy consulting-style cases (MECE frameworks, market sizing, competitive analysis)
- 🌍 **Strong customer empathy** — Revolut culture demands obsession with customer experience
- 💡 **Show product instinct** — bring examples of 0-1 product thinking from personal projects or prior internships
- 📊 **Quantifiable impact** — every project should have measurable outcomes
- 🔍 **Don't memorise frameworks** — interviewers want creative, original thinking
