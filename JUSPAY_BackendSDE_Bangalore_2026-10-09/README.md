## ✅ Eligibility & Selection

### Eligibility
- 🎓 **2027 Bachelor's or Master's graduate** in CS / IT / related
- 📅 **DSA + functional programming (Haskell/Rust preferred) + systems**
- 💻 **Open-source contributions a plus** (JUSPAY open-sources many tools)
