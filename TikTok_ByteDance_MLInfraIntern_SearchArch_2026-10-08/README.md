## What You'll Build (ML Infra, Search Arch Team)

> *"Work with ML Infra and algorithm teams on training and inference for search models."*

You will specialize in **at least one** of the following:

### 1️⃣ LLM / VLM Inference Optimization

- 🧠 **Inference frameworks:** SGLang, vLLM, TensorRT-LLM
- ⚡ **KV cache optimization**, **continuous batching**
- 🚀 **FlashAttention** kernels
- 💻 Optimize inference throughput/latency for production search models

### 2️⃣ Distributed Training for Large Models

- 🏋️ **SFT (Supervised Fine-Tuning)**, **distillation**, **RL post-training**
- ⚙️ **FSDP** (Fully Sharded Data Parallel), **multi-GPU performance tuning**
- 📊 Scaling training across thousands of GPUs

### 3️⃣ GPU Operators & Kernels

- 🔧 **CUDA**, **Triton kernels** writing
- 📈 **Profiling & bottleneck analysis** (NSight, etc.)
- ⚡ Custom kernel optimization for attention, normalization, etc.
