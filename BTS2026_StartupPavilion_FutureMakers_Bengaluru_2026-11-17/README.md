## ✅ Eligibility & Requirements for Startup POD

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Entity Type** | Registered company in India (Pvt Ltd / LLP / OPC / Partnership) |
| **Age Limit** | Less than **7 years old** since incorporation |
| **GST Registration** | Required (Registered or Unregistered both options, but GST Status: "Unregistered (Not Available)" suggests Registered is mandatory) |
| **PAN Card** | Company PAN mandatory |
| **TAN Number** | TAN mandatory for TDS compliance |
| **Documents** | Company Registration Certificate (PDF, max 2MB) |
| **GST Status** | GST Registered (with GST number) |
| **Industry Sector** | Must select from dropdown (Startup, IT, AI, Fintech, Healthtech, EdTech, etc.) |
| **Subsector** | Must specify (AI & ML, IoT, AR/VR, Blockchain, etc.) |
| **Sales Executive** | Must select from BTS sales team dropdown |
| **Currency** | INR (Indian) or USD (International) |

### ⚠️ Important for Student Founders with Just an Idea

**You cannot directly apply with just an idea.** You MUST:
1. ✅ **Register a company first** (Pvt Ltd or LLP — costs ~₹5K–₹15K via CA)
2. ✅ **Apply for GST registration** (can be done voluntarily even with no revenue)
3. ✅ **Apply for PAN + TAN** (auto with company registration)
4. ✅ **Get DPIIT Startup India recognition** (free, online, ~7 days)
5. ✅ **Build MVP / working prototype** before Nov 2026
6. ✅ **Then apply for BTS Startup POD**

## 💡 Stand-Out Tips for the Startup Pavilion

1. ✅ **Have a live demo** — not just slides. Investors skip static booths.
2. ✅ **5-second elevator pitch ready** — most visitors decide quickly
3. ✅ **Print 200+ one-pagers** (PDF or physical) to hand out
4. ✅ **Bring a TV/monitor** if budget allows (visual demos convert 3x)
5. ✅ **Wear branded T-shirts** (your startup logo) for the team
6. ✅ **Set up InterlinX meetings BEFORE the event** (slots fill fast)
7. ✅ **Have a clear ask** (funding amount, partnership type, pilot scope)
8. ✅ **Demo a real problem solved** — not "we plan to..."
9. ✅ **Bring a cofounder/team** — solo founders look less serious
10. ✅ **Follow up within 48 hrs** with everyone you meet
