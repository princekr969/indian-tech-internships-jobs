## Selection Process (Amazon Standard)

| Stage | Format | Duration | What It Tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Resume Screen** | Amazon.jobs portal | — | Linux + Python/Java/Go + projects |
| **2. Online Assessment (OA)** | HackerShell | 90-120 min | 2-3 DSA coding problems + debugging + work-style assessment |
| **3. Phone/Video Screen** | Amazon Chime | 60 min | 1-2 coding problems + DSA |
| **5. Onsite (Virtual)** | 4-5 rounds | 4-5 hours | (a) 2-3 coding rounds, (b) 1 system design, (c) 1 behavioural (Leadership Principles), (d) 1 hiring manager |
| **6. Bar Raiser Round** | Bar-raiser interview | 60 min | Cultural + LP bar |
| **7. Offer** | Via recruiting team | — | Compensation + start date |

**Total timeline:** 4-8 weeks.

### Amazon-Specific Interview Tips

- 🎯 **Leadership Principles (LPs) are a non-negotiable gate** — prepare 10-12 STAR stories covering all 16 LPs
- 💻 Coding rounds emphasize **clean, working solutions** over clever tricks
- 🏗️ System design at SDE-I level focuses on **HLD + LLD basics**
- 🐼 Should evaluate **Linux + scripting + automation depth** (this role leans SysDev, not pure SWE)
