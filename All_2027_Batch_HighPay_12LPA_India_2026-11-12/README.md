### The honest headline, before any list

> **The median fresher package in India is ₹3.0–4.5 LPA.** That figure comes from AmbitionBox, Glassdoor and LinkedIn salary-insights aggregations, and it is the number every mass-recruit drive actually pays.

| Hiring channel | Typical fresh-grad package | Clears 12 LPA? |
|---|---|---|
| **Mass-recruit IT services** — TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, Accenture, Capgemini, HCLTech, LTIMindtree | **₹3.5 – 9 LPA** | ❌ **No** |
| **Mid-tier product companies** — Zoho, Freshworks, Persistent, Globant, Hexaware | **₹6 – 10 LPA** | ❌ No |
| **Indian product startups** — Razorpay, Zerodha, CRED, PhonePe, Zepto, Postman | **₹12 – 25 LPA** | ✅ Sometimes |
| **Global product & FAANG-adjacent** — Microsoft, Amazon, Adobe, Salesforce, Nvidia, Cisco, Google | **₹16 – 35 LPA** | ✅ Usually |
| **FAANG / top-tier** — Google, Meta | **₹30 – 50+ LPA** | ✅ Yes |

> ⚠️ **So the strategy is forced, not optional.** If the target is ₹12+ LPA, **the mass-recruit drives are your fallback, not your plan.** TCS NQT, Infosys SE, Cognizant GenC, Wipro Elite and Accenture ASE together pay **₹3.5–9 LPA**. Every one of them is open, several close this month, and every one of them will be far easier to get into than a product-company role. **Apply to the mass-recruit drives as insurance, and spend your DSA hours on the product-company list in Tier 2.**

> 🔥 **One deadline today.** **TCS iON NQT's November cycle closes on 12 Nov 2026 — that is today.** The test is 24 Nov 2026. It is ₹999 (IT) / ₹599 (non-IT), it accepts **2022–2028 pass-outs in any stream**, and it is the gate to TCS BPS, Digital and Prime hiring. It does not clear 12 LPA — but it is cheap, and a TCS NQT score is read by other employers too.

## 📋 Eligible Batches & Fit

> **Honest fit assessment.** A 2027-batch student is in **4th year / 7th semester** in November 2026 and graduates in **May/June 2027**. Above ₹12 LPA the fit is far sharper than in the general market, because the product companies that pay those bands recruit **only the current graduating cohort** — and that cohort is 2027.

## 📋 Eligible Branches / Disciplines

> **Honest fit assessment.** Above ₹12 LPA the branch filter is real but wider than it looks. Product companies hire by **discipline family and skills**, not by a hard branch list, and several of the highest bands on this list are hardware or quant roles that a CS student cannot take — while several that look CS-only are open to ECE.

| Branch / Discipline | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Computer Science (CS / CSE)** | ✅ **Strongest** | Software, backend, full-stack, data, ML and platform roles dominate this list — Stripe, Razorpay, PhonePe, CRED, Swiggy, Adobe, Salesforce, Cisco |
| **Information Technology (IT)** | ✅ Strong | Indistinguishable from CS for these roles; accepted in the standard “B.Tech / BCA / MCA” eligibility lines |
| **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** | ✅ Strong | AI/ML new-grad roles carry the top bands — NVIDIA, Observe.ai, Innovaccer, Adobe |
| **Machine Learning (ML)** | ✅ Strong | ML internships, data-science and applied-AI roles; several explicitly list AI / ML / Data Science as the accepted degree |
| **Data Science (DS)** | ✅ Strong | Dedicated data / analytics new-grad roles across the product set |
| **Electronics & Communication (ECE)** | ✅ **Genuinely strong here** | **Qualcomm's 2027 Campus Hire family is half hardware** — *Engineer HW* and *System Engineer* tracks sit alongside the SW tracks. **Google's Silicon Engineer (₹33–47 LPA)** and **Meta's ASIC Engineer (₹34 LPA)** are hardware roles open to ECE. Salesforce AMTS lists CS, ECE and EE together |
| **Electrical Engineering (EE)** | ⚠️ Possible | Silicon, embedded, VLSI and ASIC tracks only — Meta ASIC, Google Silicon, Qualcomm HW |
| **Mathematics (Math)** | ⚠️ Possible | Quant and actuarial roles; **Graviton's Software Engineer (₹35 LPA+)** explicitly accepts CS, Mathematics and Electrical Engineering |

> 📌 Each role in Tier 1 carries its **stated qualification** verbatim, and the hardware-heavy roles are called out in the notes so a CS student does not burn an application on an ASIC position.

## 🧭 The 12+ LPA Playbook for a 2027 Batch

> A 2027-batch student chasing ₹12+ LPA has **seven months** and a very specific sequence. The failure mode is not “applied to too few” — it is **“spent three weeks on TCS NQT preparation and then bombed the product-company interview because the DSA was shallow.”**

```
  2027-BATCH (2023-2027) - 4th year / 7th semester, Nov 2026
  ===========================================================================

  NOV 2026  (NOW - two hard deadlines this month)
    |-- Register TCS iON NQT TODAY (closes 12 Nov, test 24 Nov, Rs 999)
    |-- Apply Salesforce AMTS + Cisco + Qualcomm 2027 Campus Hire
    |-- Apply GE Vernova Digital Technology Development Program
    |-- Apply 8-10 product-company early-career portals (Tier 2)
    |-- Start the ONE prep that matters: DSA + system design, 3 problems/day
    `-- State Street R-800791 by 30 Nov (cheap to try, bank-tech name)

  DEC 2026 - JAN 2027
    |-- TCS NQT on 24 Nov -> NQT score + BPS/Digital/Prime routes
    |-- Re-apply Tier 2 portals (they accept rolling applications)
    |-- Product-company interviews: DSA + system design + 'why this company'
    `-- Apply to 6+ rolling ATS portals (insurance, not target)

  FEB - APR 2027
    |-- Product-company rounds continue; referrals matter more here
    |-- Final-year internship with a PPO at a product company
    `-- Keep every portal live; re-submit

  MAY - JUN 2027
    `-- Join, or enter the next cycle with a PPO in hand
  ```

### The four things that decide a ₹12+ LPA outcome for a 2027 batch

| Lever | Why it matters specifically above the 12 LPA line |
|---|---|
| **DSA must be genuinely strong — this is the gate, not the gatekeeper** | Every product company in Tier 2 screens with a DSA test before a human sees you. TCS NQT's coding section is *easier* than a Google or Razorpay screen. Preparing only for NQT and then walking into a Razorpay interview is the classic way to end up with a ₹4 LPA offer and a 12 LPA plan. |
| **Apply on day one of a posting, and apply directly to portals** | Product-company postings are often live for only 7–14 days (the community directory says exactly this). The portals are year-round. Registering on the portal the day a listing appears is the difference between a 3-day-old posting and a 12-day-old one. |
| **A referral or a warm contact is worth 3–5× a cold application** | At ₹12+ LPA the applicant pool is mostly IIT/NIT/IIIT/BITS students with referrals and strong resumes. The public salary compilations say outright that these packages “mostly go to IIT/NIT/IIIT/BITS students”. **From a non-top-tier college it is possible, but it needs strong DSA and off-campus application volume** — ABV-IIITM Gwalior is an NIRF Top-100 IIIT, which is a strong signal, but it is not a substitute for referral or DSA. |
| **Two applications to the same company beat one application to two** | Product companies hire in **teams**, and a team lead who wants you can pull you into a second opening. After a first interview, ask for feedback and stay in the pipeline — the second opening usually pays the same and has less competition. |

### Seven traps in this brief, stated plainly

1. **Infosys SP L2 / L3 (₹16 L / ₹21 LPA) is not open to a 2027 batch.** Batch-locked to 2024–2026. It is the most-shared “2027 batch 20 LPA” claim online and it is wrong.
2. **A “package (company band)” figure is crowd-compiled, not official.** No product company publishes its fresher band. The Tier 2 table is a *ranking aid*; the Tier 1 figures are quoted from actual postings. Trust Tier 1 for numbers, Tier 2 for where to look.
3. **A monthly stipend annualised is not a package.** ₹1,20,000/month is ₹14.4 LPA annualised — but a 6-month internship is **₹7.2 LPA of actual money**, and only if it converts. Read the PPO clause before treating an internship as a 12 LPA offer.
4. **Wipro Elite NTH status is genuinely contradictory right now.** The official page has no NTH section; some trackers say open, others say closed. And it pays ₹3.5 LPA anyway. **Open `careers.wipro.com/elite` yourself and decide from what you see** — do not forward-fill your decision from a WhatsApp message.
5. **“Actively Hiring” on LinkedIn is a marketing tag, not a vacancy count.** It was applied to the postings in this brief as a *tie-breaker*, never as proof of an open role. Every link here was re-fetched and answered on 12 Nov 2026 instead.
6. **You cannot search LinkedIn for a salary.** The search box matches the posting **title**, not the description — typing *18 LPA* returns the same generic result set as *12 LPA* and *30 LPA*. The package lives in the job description, so it only shows up when you open the posting. **If a site claims you can filter LinkedIn jobs by package, that is a paid aggregator, not LinkedIn.**
7. **A wide package band is not the same as a 12 LPA offer.** Kulfi is quoted *9–15 LPA*, Onsurity *9–14 LPA*, Zebra *9–13 LPA*, Teesta *8–15 LPA*. The ceiling clears the line; the floor does not. Read the whole band before you count a role as a win.

## ⚠️ Caveats & Method

### What was done

```
  1. LINKEDIN  (guest job-search API, no login)
     - sweep A: 923 queries = 21 package/CTC keywords + 10 batch keywords
       + 90 high-payer companies x 9 role angles + 17 city hubs x 3
       -> 8,010 unique job IDs across 2,505 companies
     - sweep B (title-focused): 40 fresher / 2027-batch / campus / graduate phrases
       x entry-level filters x 2 pagination offsets -> 420 further unique job IDs
     - filters: f_E=1,2 (entry level), f_TPR=r2592000 (last 30 days), geoId=India
     -> 760 full job descriptions were fetched and parsed (the survivors after India + CS/IT + entry-level filtering)

     A hard limit worth knowing: the guest search endpoint matches `keywords` against the
     posting TITLE, not the description. A query like '18 LPA' therefore returns a generic
     result set instead of package-matching postings - there is no way to search LinkedIn
     for a salary. **The package can only be read out of the JD body itself**, which is why
     the package figures below come from the postings, never from the search API.

  2. PACKAGE EXTRACTION  (multi-form, not a single regex)
     '18 LPA' | '9-15 LPA' | '35 LPA +' | '18 lakhs per annum'
     'CTC of 15.5 lakhs' | 'Rs 20,00,000 per annum' | '30,000/ Month'
     -> monthly stipends annualised at x12; threshold applied as STRICTLY > 12 LPA

  3. LIVENESS  - every link in this brief was opened on 12 Nov 2026 and its HTTP status recorded. 200/301/302 = live; 403/405/406/429 = live but bot-guarded (a WAF that a real browser passes, flagged rather than silently counted as live); 404/410 = dead.
     Where a company's own site could not be reached from this machine, the row says so and points at the listing instead of pretending to a clean apply link.

  4. BATCH+PACKAGE INDEX  - thejobcompany.co.in 2027-batch pages: 40 pages,
     466 unique entries, each with batch / location / qualification / package.
     23 cleared the 12 LPA threshold; each one's apply link resolved and re-verified.

  5. PORTALS  - 75 official career portals fetched on 12 Nov 2026; 68 returned HTTP 200 on a re-check and 6 were bot-guarded (403/429 - reachable in a browser). Every one of them is listed in Tier 2 with its final (redirected) URL and its actual status on the day.

  6. DRIVES  - 16 mass-recruit drives status-checked on 12 Nov 2026 against official
     pages and dated trackers, with the source named on every row.
```

### What this brief is not

| Limitation | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Package bands are crowd-compiled** | Tier 2's bands come from 2026 public compilations, not company disclosure. Tier 1's figures are quoted from actual postings. The two tiers are not the same kind of evidence and the brief labels them separately |
| **LinkedIn's guest API caps results** | Roughly the top 25–50 per query, de-duplicated by pagination. Product-company campus roles are under-represented — Google, Meta, Stripe and NVIDIA publish on their own portals, not on LinkedIn. That is exactly why Tier 2 exists |
| **“Live” is a point-in-time check** | Every link was opened on **12 Nov 2026**. A drive or portal can close without announcement. **Re-check before you submit.** |
| **Aggregator listings are second-hand** | Tier 1's thejobcompany rows are listings, not employer pages — the apply link in each row is the employer's own form, which is why those links resolve to ServiceNow / Workday / company domains |
| **Eligibility gaps exist** | Some postings name a batch but leave branch, CGPA or backlog rules unstated. Where the listing states a qualification it is quoted verbatim; where it is silent, that is what the row says |
| **Stipend annualisation is arithmetic, not a promise** | A ₹1,20,000/month internship is not a ₹14.4 LPA job. Treat Tier 1B as a route to a PPO, not as a package |
| **This is a CS/IT brief** | The hardware and quant roles are included because they clear the threshold and a 2027 ECE student may want them — each is labelled with its stated qualification |
