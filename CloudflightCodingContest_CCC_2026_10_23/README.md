## About Cloudflight + CCC

- 🏢 **Cloudflight** (formerly Catalysts GmbH) — global digital solutions provider
- 🏆 **CCC running since 2007** — one of the longest-running coding competitions globally
- 🌐 **42nd Edition** in 2026 — heritage that goes back nearly two decades
- 🌍 **Hybrid format** — online + 30+ on-site locations in 10+ countries
- 🎯 Built to "**drive technological innovation**" through multi-level algorithmic + ML challenges

## What You'll Do

> *"Each year, thousands of coders from all around the world simultaneously compete against each other in level-based coding games. With each level the difficulty increases and the individual contestant (or a team) reaching the most difficult level within the shortest time wins."*

- 🧩 Solve **multi-level algorithmic puzzles** (Classic)
- 📊 Build ML models (Data/AI track)
- 🏆 Compete for **highest level reached in shortest time**
- 🎖️ Earn badges based on performance
- 📍 Compete from anywhere in the world via online contest

- 💻 Practice on **CatCoder** — the official CCC platform
- 💬 Join **Discord** for tips, updates, community chats
- 🎯 Solve past CCC contests for difficulty calibration
- 🧠 Train for **6 levels of increasing difficulty**

## Apply Tip

- 🎯 **Start at the #Classic track** (most accessible)
- 📚 **Practice 1-2 weeks in advance** — 6 levels is non-trivial
- 👥 **Form a team of 3** — solo is harder but doable
- ⏱️ **Time management** — first 3 levels should be fast (≤30 min each), reserve last 3 for the second half
- 🧮 **Master common algorithms** before contest: BFS, DFS, shortest paths, dynamic programming, greedy
- 🌐 **Online venue "The Web"** is your safest bet from India (no on-site venue confirmed for 2026 yet)
