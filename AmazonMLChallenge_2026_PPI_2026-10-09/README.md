> **Top 50 teams** win **Pre-Placement Interviews (PPIs)** for the **Applied Scientist Intern** role at Amazon — direct interview pathway to one of the most coveted intern roles at Amazon (₹60K-80K/mo + PPO).

## Eligibility

### Who Can Participate

- ✅ **Engineering students at Indian engineering campuses**
- ✅ **B.E. / B.Tech / M.Tech / M.S. / MS by Research / PhD** (full-time programs)
- ✅ **Graduating batch: 2027 or 2028**
- ✅ Must have valid **AWS Builder Center Profile ID**
- ✅ Form a **team of 3-4 members** (cross-college teams allowed)
- ✅ Every team must have a team leader
- ✅ Each participant can be in only one team

### What You'll Do

- 📊 **Build an ML model** to solve a real-world business problem from Amazon
- ⏱️ **72-hour sprint** (3 days of intense building)
- 🤝 Cross-college teams allowed
- 💻 Online or in-person (Bangalore)

## Selection Process

| Stage | Format | Duration | What It Tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Team Registration** | AWS Builder Center | — | Form team + register |
| **2. 72-hour ML Build** | Online (anywhere) + in-person (Bangalore) | 72 hours | ML model + business value |
| **3. Judging** | Amazon panel + AWS team | — | Model performance + business impact |
| **4. Top 50 Selection** | Internal | — | Top 50 teams get PPIs |
| **5. PPI for Applied Scientist Intern** | Amazon interview process | — | Direct pathway to Amazon intern |

## Why It's Special

- 🚀 **Fast-track to Amazon** — direct interview, no resume screening
- 🤖 **ML focus** — fits students interested in ML/AI/Data Science
- 💼 **Amazon badge on resume** — applies even if you don't win top 50
- 🎓 **Skill badges + certificates** — boost LinkedIn profile
- 💰 **AWS credits** — useful for future ML projects
- 🛍️ **Amazon SWAG** — stickers, t-shirts, etc.

## Apply Tip

- 👥 **Form a 3-4 person team** ASAP — find classmates/friends
- 🧠 **Designate 1-2 ML specialists** (ML fundamentals, training loops, model selection)
- 🛠️ **Designate 1 engineer** (data pipelines, deployment, infrastructure)
- 📚 **Designate 1 domain expert / coordinator** (storytelling, presentation)
- ⏰ **Plan the 72 hours** — sleep schedule, role division, dataset exploration
- 🤝 **AWS credits** = use AWS SageMaker / Bedrock for fast iteration
- 🧪 **Test before submitting** — leaderboard freezes at 72-hour mark
