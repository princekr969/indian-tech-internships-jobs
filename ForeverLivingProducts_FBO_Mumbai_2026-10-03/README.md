There is **no fixed salary, no PF, no gratuity, no employee benefits** — only commission on sales and on sales made by people you recruit. ## 1. What the company does

Forever Living Products (FLP) is the world's largest grower, manufacturer and distributor of aloe vera products, and also sells bee-derived personal-care, nutritional and cosmetic items. The full chain — plantation → manufacturing → bottling → distribution → sale — is owned by the company ("vertically integrated"). Aloe is harvested from FLP's own fields in South Texas and the Dominican Republic, and processed at the Aloe Vera of America plant. Forever's aloe gel is certified by the International Aloe Science Council for purity and inner-leaf content.

**The catch:** distribution does **not** happen through retail stores in the traditional sense. Products reach end-consumers almost entirely through a **network of independent distributors** (FBOs) who buy at a discount and resell, and who earn overrides on the sales of distributors they sponsor.

## 2. Founder and history

- **1978:** Rex Maughan, then VP at the Del Webb Corporation (real estate), started selling aloe vera products from his garage in Tempe, Arizona with ~USD 10,000 of his own money and ~40 founding distributors. Year-one sales: ~USD 700,000.
- **1980:** Maughan quit Del Webb to run FLP full-time.
- **1981:** Acquired Aloe Vera of America's patents, cosmetics plant and field operations.
- **1983:** A deep freeze destroyed the entire Texas crop. Maughan responded by buying land in the Dominican Republic and Mexico.
- **1985:** Revenue exceeded USD 100 million.
- **1990s:** Expanded rapidly into Asia, Europe and Latin America. Hit USD 1 billion in revenue by 1995.
- **1996:** IRS (USA) and Japan's NTA jointly audited Maughan, Aloe Vera of America, Selective Art Inc., FLP International and FLP Japan for tax years 1991–1995.
- **2008:** Gregg Maughan (Rex's son) became President.
- **2010s:** Expanded global footprint beyond 165 countries.
- **17 July 2021:** Founder Rex Maughan passed away in Arizona at age 84.
- **January 2023:** Aidan O'Hare became President; Gregg Maughan continued as CEO.
- **April 2026:** Reached a settlement with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission over deceptive income claims — see Section 9.

The company also ran a **Forever Resorts** division in its earlier decades (owning and operating resorts near Grand Canyon, Grand Tetons, Bryce Canyon, Isle Royale, and even Southfork Ranch from the TV show "Dallas"). That division has shrunk over time.

### Indian regulatory framework

Forever Living operates under the **Consumer Protection (Direct Selling) Rules, 2021** issued by the Department of Consumer Affairs, Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and PDistribution, Government of India. As an FBO-distributor, the following protections exist:

- **No joining fee.** Becoming an FBO is free on paper.
- **No annual fee, no renewal fee, no training fee.**
- **30-day cooling-off period.** Any FBO can cancel within 30 days of joining and return any resalable, unused product in good condition for a full refund (Voluntary Termination clause 17.08).
- **KYC required:** proof of identity, proof of address, PAN card.
- **Adults only:** 18+ years, Indian resident.

## 6. How "joining" actually works

There is **no employee onboarding** because there are no employees. The path is:

### Step 1 — Preferred Customer (free)

- Register at foreverliving.com with name, address, PAN, age proof.
- Get a **Preferred Customer ID**.
- Allowed to place orders at a **15% discount** off the Bonus Recap Price (BRP).
- Maximum **~INR 31,000 of product per calendar month** until KYC is verified.
- Cannot earn team commissions or sponsor anyone.

### Step 2 — Forever Business Owner / FBO (still free on paper)

- Submit FBO application with KYC documents.
- Get FBO ID.
- Entitled to buy product at **5% discount** initially.
- Can sell to customers at full BRP and keep the difference (retail profit).
- Once sales turnover reaches **INR 5,000**, can register a Preferred Customer and start earning **25% on that customer's purchases**.

### Step 3 — Wholesale Qualified FBO + Assistant Supervisor (this is where it costs real money)

- To unlock team commissions and higher bonuses, the FBO must complete **2 Case Credits (2 CC) in two consecutive months**.
- In India, **1 CC ≈ INR 13,200 – 15,000** of product purchased at wholesale.
- **2 CC ≈ INR 26,400 – 30,000** of upfront product purchase — this is the de-facto "entry cost".
- On hitting 2 CC, you become a **Wholesale Qualified FBO and Assistant Supervisor**:
  - **30% discount** on personal purchases (instead of 5%).
  - **5% Personal Bonus** on personal / non-WHQ FBO purchases.
  - **5% Preferred Customer Bonus** on personally-sponsored customers' purchases.
  - Can now actively build a downline of FBOs.

### Practical first-order checklist (per FLP India's FAQ)

- Minimum first-order size for FBO: **INR 1,000 worth of products** including taxes.
- Delivery: INR 125 per invoice up to 1 CC; INR 250 per invoice above 1 CC.
- Orders placed only via the **My Forever India app** or **www.aloestoreindia.in** — never pay cash to any individual.
- ID card issued free of cost.

### What the company explicitly promises

- No joining fees, no annual fees, no renewal fees, no training fees.
- A 30-day cooling-off period with full refund on resalable stock.
- You are an independent distributor, not an employee.

### What the company does **not** promise

- No fixed salary.
- No minimum income.
- No guarantee that you'll recoup your starter purchase.
- No PF / ESI / gratuity / health insurance / paid leave.
- Income depends "solely on the sale of company products" and recruitment of a downline.

## 7. Compensation plan (sales levels and incomes)

Once you are Wholesale Qualified and an Assistant Supervisor, the path upward is measured in **Case Credits** (CC) generated by you + your downline.

| Rank | CC required (India) | Personal discount | Personal Bonus on BRP | What's unlocked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Preferred Customer | – | 15% | – | Personal-use discount only |
| **Assistant Supervisor** | 2 CC in 2 consecutive months | 30% + 5% | 5% to +5% | First level team commissions |
| **Supervisor** | 25 CC in 2 consecutive months | 30% | 8% | Volume bonus on Supervisors |
| **Assistant Manager** | 75 CC in 2 consecutive months (revised Jan 2025) | 30% | 13% | 5% on downline Supervisors |
| **Senior / Soaring / Sapphire / Diamond Manager** | Manager + N downline Managers | 30% | 18% | Leadership bonuses, car plan |

The 11 "incomes" the company talks about in India:

1. Retail Profit (margin between wholesale and MRP)
2. Personal Bonus
3. Volume Bonus
4. Leadership Bonus

7. Profit Sharing

**Company's headline number to recruits:** "up to **43% profit** on a sale to a new person." This is the *theoretical maximum* combining discount + personal bonus + preferred customer bonus. Actual realised profit per rupee of turnover is much lower.

## 8. Income reality

This is the section to read twice. The company publishes an **Income Disclosure Statement** on its website, and the U.S. Federal Trade Commission filed a lawsuit in April 2026 based on data the company itself produced.

### Official FLP global income disclosure (most recent annual data, USD)

Of all "monthly purchasers" globally:

- **89.8% received no meaningful compensation or earnings** in an average month.
- Of the **10.2%** who earned a volume-based bonus, plus retail profit:
  - **41.4%** earned **more than USD 500/year** from downline sales (excluding personal retail profit).
  - Of that group:
    - **69%** earned average **USD 111/month** (≈ INR 9,300/month; ≈ INR 1.1 lakh/year).
    - **30%** earned average **USD 1,670/month** (≈ INR 14 lakh/year).
    - **Top <1%** earned average **USD 31,235/month** (≈ INR 2.6 crore/year).
- **88.6%** of monthly buyers globally did not earn a bonus at all.

These figures are **bonuses only**. They do **not** include:
- Personal expenses (samples, travel, hosting meetings, marketing materials).
- Mandatory monthly purchases to remain "active" and qualify for bonuses.
- Inventory the FBO may have bought and been unable to sell.
- Taxes.

### India-specific anecdotal data (AmbitionBox, Glassdoor)

Based on self-reported salaries from distributors / FBOs:

| Role | Avg annual income | Range |
|---|---|---|
| Distributor | INR 14,887 – 50,000 | Very low |
| Assistant Supervisor | INR 2.4 – 2.8 lakh | INR 0.4 – 7.4 lakh |
| Supervisor | INR 3.5 – 3.8 lakh | INR 1 – 8 lakh |
| Assistant Manager | INR 4.1 lakh | INR 1.5 – 7.5 lakh |
| Manager | INR 8 – 12 lakh | Top range |

Caveats: AmbitionBox/Glassdoor salaries are **self-reported, voluntarily submitted**, and skewed toward people who bothered to log in. They are not the same as audited income distribution.

### What people commonly lose

- **Initial product purchase to become Assistant Supervisor:** INR 26,000 – 30,000 (2 CC).
- **Subsequent monthly purchase requirement** to remain "active" and qualify for full bonus.
- **Time spent** in webinars, meetings, training, recruiting — often 2–5 hours/day.
- **Personal relationships** strained by constant recruitment pitches to friends and family.
- **Inventory that cannot be sold** at MRP because customers can buy similar aloe products elsewhere cheaper.

## 9. FTC action (April 2026) — important context

On **14 April 2026**, the U.S. Federal Trade Commission and Forever Living Products International LLC, Forever Living.com LLC, CEO Gregg Maughan and President Aidan O'Hare entered into a **stipulated final order** filed in U.S. District Court for the District of Arizona.

What the FTC alleged (based on the company's own internal data):

- In each of the last five years, **at least 77%** of FBOs who purchased, sold or recruited did not receive any compensation.
- **More than 89%** of new participants had not, even after two full years, earned enough from FLP to recoup their initial **USD 300+ startup cost**.
- **Less than 7%** of FBOs received income from their downline's sales/purchases.
- For years, FLP's public Income Disclosure Statement implied non-earning participants had "elected not to participate in the Marketing Plan," when the company knew nearly 90% of FBOs received no income.
- The FTC catalogued **5,500+ atypical / deceptive income claims** made directly by FLP and its executives (e.g., "full-time income," "uncapped income," "financial freedom").

What the order requires Forever Living to do (in the United States):

- Maintain substantiation for any earnings claim and provide it to any U.S. consumer who asks.
- Stop misrepresenting participants' earnings.
- Stop misrepresenting the reasons participants don't earn (e.g., "they're not trying").
- Stop misrepresenting the likelihood of recruiting others.

Concurrent company policy update (effective **1 May 2026**, US only):

- FLP announced it will **no longer allow distributors in the U.S. to recruit new participants**.
- Until end of 2026, existing US downlines can still earn from "existing downline purchasing."
- After 2026, US operations shift to "a model centered on product sales and long-term compliance."

**Implication for India (as of October 2026):**

- The MLM structure is **still fully operational in India** — FLP India's Company Policy (April 2026 version) still references all four Sales Levels and downline commissions.
- However, the FTC case is a **major warning signal** for any prospective FBO worldwide. The company's own data — not critics' — shows ~90% of participants earn nothing.
- Anyone considering joining in India should assume the same income distribution applies globally.

## 10. Pros, cons and red flags

### Pros

- Aloe-based products are **real, tangible goods** — not a token or a fictitious service.
- Company is **vertically integrated** and has been operating for **48 years** without going under.
- Aloe vera content is **IASC-certified** (International Aloe Science Council).
- Products generally considered **safe** as food supplements / cosmetics.
- Joining is free on paper; 30-day cooling-off period is real.
- Discount of 15% (Preferred Customer) up to 30% (Wholesale Qualified FBO) on personal purchases.
- If one genuinely enjoys the products and uses them personally, the discount is real value.
- Indian regulatory framework (Direct Selling Rules 2021) provides consumer protection.

### Cons

- **No salary, no employee benefits.** You are self-employed; you bear all risk.
- **~90% of participants globally receive no compensation** per the company's own disclosure.
- **Median earnings for the 30% who do earn:** roughly INR 9,000–11,000/month — before expenses.
- **Top 1% capture the vast majority of income.**
- **Mandatory monthly product purchase** to remain "active" — this is a real recurring cost.
- **Recruitment is the actual business**, not product sales. Friends and family become the primary customer base.
- **Inventory write-off risk:** unsold stock cannot easily be returned except within the 30-day window.
- **Income disclosure has historically been inflated**; FTC found 5,500+ deceptive claims made by the company itself.
- **Social-media recruitment pitches** (Instagram "work from home" reels, YouTube webinars charging INR 196–299) are a major grievance.
- **Health-claim restrictions:** products are supplements, not medicines. Marketing them as cures is illegal.
- **Regulatory risk:** the U.S. has just forced a structural shift; other countries could follow.

### Red flags to watch for in any sponsor

- Promising a specific monthly income ("guaranteed INR 20,000–30,000/month").
- Charging for "masterclasses" or "webinars" before revealing it's FLP (Reddit users report INR 196–299 fees).
- Asking you to recruit a minimum number of downline members per month.
- Pressuring you to buy more product to "qualify" for the next rank.
- Showing photos of cars / international trips / cheque presentations as proof of earnings.
- Asking for cash / UPI transfers to a personal account (always pay the company through aloestoreindia.in or the My Forever India app).
- Discouraging you from reading the Income Disclosure Statement or talking to family before joining.
- Refusing to give you the company's official Company Policy document.

## 11. Checklist before signing up

Use this before transferring any money or signing any application form.

- [ ] Have I read the **Income Disclosure Statement** at https://foreverliving.com/ind/en-us/income-disclosure?
- [ ] Have I read the full **Company Policy** (April 2026 version) available from the Indian site?
- [ ] Have I spoken to **at least 3 existing FBOs of different ranks** (not just my sponsor) and asked about their actual net income after expenses?
- [ ] Have I asked the sponsor for their **own Income Disclosure Statement printout** showing their rank, monthly bonus and date they joined?
- [ ] Do I understand that **2 CC ≈ INR 26,000–30,000** of product purchase is the real "entry cost", even though joining is free?
- [ ] Am I prepared to spend **2–5 hours/day** on marketing, meetings and follow-ups for 6–12 months with no guaranteed return?
- [ ] Have I checked that the sponsor's FBO ID is real on the official site?
- [ ] Am I clear that I am joining as an **independent distributor**, not an employee — and that I am responsible for my own GST, income tax and business expenses?
- [ ] Have I confirmed the **30-day cooling-off period** and the **buy-back policy** for resalable products?
- [ ] Have I set a **personal stop-loss** (e.g., "I will exit if I have not earned back my initial purchase within 12 months")?

## 13. How to find the right sponsor

The person who introduces you (your sponsor) determines a large part of your experience, because they handle your training, your upline support, and your early customer introductions.

**Where to look:**

1. **Official site:** foreverliving.com/ind/en-us/welcome — but you will need a sponsor's FBO ID to register.
2. **FLP India social media (verified):** Facebook (flpindia), Instagram (flp.india), LinkedIn (Forever Living Products India).
3. **LinkedIn search:** "Forever Living" + India — many FBOs list themselves as "Independent Distributor at Forever Living Products." Check how long they've been active and how their network looks.
4. **YouTube / Instagram webinar hosts** — treat with caution; the FTC action specifically criticised FLP's social-media marketing style.
6. **In-person events:** Forever India occasionally hosts large meetings in Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore; the company has a new Mumbai office (Ackruti Star, Andheri East).

**How to vet a sponsor — questions to ask in writing:**

- What is your rank, and since when?
- What was your net personal income from FLP in the last full financial year (after subtracting your own purchases)?
- How many active downline FBOs do you have right now?
- Can you send me the Company Policy and the Income Disclosure Statement as PDFs?
- Are you willing to commit in writing that there is no minimum purchase beyond the 2 CC qualification?
- Do you have any history with the Direct Selling Rules 2021 complaints or any pending case?
- Can I attend two of your team meetings **before** committing any money?

**Sponsors to avoid:**

- Anyone who quotes a guaranteed monthly income.
- Anyone who charges for "training," "webinars," "masterclasses" or "starter kits" paid to a personal account.
- Anyone who discourages you from reading the Income Disclosure Statement.
- Anyone who promises that "within 6 months you'll be at Manager rank."
