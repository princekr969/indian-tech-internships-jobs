2. **📊 Maintain a placement dashboard** — track which companies said yes/no, response times, CTC bands
3. **🎤 Run a PPT series (Oct-Feb)** — invite 20-30 companies for pre-placement talks to build relationships
4. **💻 Subsidize competitive programming** — offer LeetCode/CodeChef premium accounts to top 30 students
5. **🏆 Run an internal hackathon** — companies that judge internal hackathons often come for placements
6. **🤝 Build a placement ambassador program** — 5-10 motivated students per dept to drive company outreach
7. **📧 Centralize communication** — single placement email ID, single WhatsApp group for recruiters
8. **🎁 Pre-place top performers early** — convert top 10-15 students to offers by Nov-Dec 2026 (before mass drives)
9. **🌐 Build a strong placement microsite** — public page with batch stats, top recruiters, past CTCs (helps pitch to new companies)
10. **🔁 Debrief after every drive** — what worked, what didn't, recruiter feedback, share with next-year T&P
