## 📚 8 Tips to Get Selected (From Pavan Joshi's Post)

1. 🎯 **Your college name or resume won't get you selected. Your contributions will.**
2. 📚 **Focus on open source, Git, and GitHub.** Understand commit conventions, SemVer, issues, PRs, reviews, release workflows.
3. 🐣 **Start with small, meaningful PRs.** Don't try a huge contribution on your first attempt.
4. 📝 **Write commits and PR descriptions from the reviewer's perspective** — make it easy to understand what you changed and why.
5. 🚫 **Don't spam orgs with dozens of PRs** just to show activity.
6. 🤖 **Avoid AI-generated PRs that don't add real value.** Maintainers can usually tell.
7. 🎯 **Don't spread across too many orgs.** One org is ideal; two should be the maximum.
8. 🔍 **Spend time understanding the project's codebase, community, contribution guidelines, and existing issues.**

> **The goal isn't to maximize your PR count.**
> **The goal is to become someone the maintainers already trust to contribute to their project.**

3. ✅ **Build trust through repeated quality PRs** — not spam
