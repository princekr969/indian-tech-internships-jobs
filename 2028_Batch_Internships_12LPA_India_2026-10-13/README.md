# Section 7 — Selection Process (Common Pattern for Tier-1 Tech Internships)

```
Resume + Application
     ↓
Online Assessment (OA)
  - 2-3 DSA questions (medium-hard)
  - 1-2 CS fundamentals MCQs
     ↓
Technical Interview Round 1
  - 1-2 DSA + 1-2 system design / LLD
     ↓
Technical Interview Round 2
  - Project deep-dive + 1 DSA + behavioral
     ↓
Hiring Manager / Culture Fit Round
     ↓
Offer
```

> **Note:** Microsoft, Google, Amazon have their own unique pipelines — verify per company.

# Section 11 — Why 12+ LPA Stipend is Achievable (Reality Check)

> *The "12+ LPA" threshold is now realistic for 2028 batch students — here's why:*

| Era | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 | 2027 onwards |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Stipend ceiling (top tier)** | ₹50-80k/month | ₹80k-1L/month | ₹1L-1.25L/month | ₹1.25L+/month expected |
| **FTE floor (top tier)** | ₹18-25 LPA | ₹20-30 LPA | ₹25-40 LPA | ₹30-50+ LPA |
| **Notable trend** | Service giants dominate | Product startups catching up | Product + global giants dominant | AI-native roles emerging |

> **Reality:** The 12+ LPA threshold is no longer "elite" — it's the **floor for top-tier internships** in 2026-2027. Top performers at IIT/BITS get 25+ LPA, but even Tier-1.5 / Tier-2 college students can land 12-18+ LPA roles through off-campus channels.
