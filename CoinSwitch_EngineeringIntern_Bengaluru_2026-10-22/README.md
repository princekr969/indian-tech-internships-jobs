## 🎯 What This Role Is About

**CoinSwitch** is **India's largest crypto investing app**, built by **PeepalCo** — a canopy of wealth-tech brands whose mission is *"Making Money Equal for All."* The group was founded by **Ashish Singhal, Govind Soni, and Vimal Sagar Tiwari**, and is backed by **Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), Tiger Global, Peak XV Partners (formerly Sequoia Capital India), Ribbit Capital, Paradigm, and Coinbase Ventures**. Its largest brands are **CoinSwitch** and **Lemonn** (trading).

As an **Engineering Intern** at CoinSwitch, you work on the **production crypto exchange infrastructure** — the backend services that match buy/sell orders, the wallet/ledger system tracking crypto balances, the KYC/onboarding flow, and the customer-facing app where people buy crypto with INR.

This is **not** a "learn React" internship. It's a **high-velocity fintech product** where your code touches real money and real users from day one.
