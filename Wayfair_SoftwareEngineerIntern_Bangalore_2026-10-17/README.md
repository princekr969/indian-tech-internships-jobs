## 🎯 What This Role Is About

**Wayfair (NYSE: W)** is the **world's largest online destination for the home** — selling furniture, décor, appliances, and home goods to **~30 million active customers** worldwide. Unlike Amazon (which sells everything), Wayfair specializes deeply in the "home" category with **22+ million products** from **20,000+ suppliers**.

Wayfair's **Bangalore office** is the company's **strategic technology delivery center** — the engineering hub for the **$50 B growth plan over the next 3-5 years**. The Bangalore team works on the same stack as Boston HQ: **AI-First applications, large-scale distributed systems, and GenAI-powered customer experiences**.

As a **Software Engineer Intern (AI-First & GenAI Focus)**, the work is **NOT a generic web dev internship** — it is **building AI-native applications** that use **Generative AI and machine learning** to redefine the customer shopping journey. This means:
- 40% building applications that integrate **LLMs (GPT, Claude, in-house models)** for personalized shopping
- 25% full-stack development (Java + Spring Boot backend, React/Next.js frontend)
- 20% data systems + ML pipelines (BigQuery, Airflow, TensorFlow/PyTorch)
- 15% cross-cutting (CI/CD, observability, code reviews)
