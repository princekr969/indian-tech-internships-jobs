## 📋 Role Details (From JD)

### About the Role
> "Tropolite is looking for enthusiastic and technically inclined students to join as Agentic AI Interns. The selected candidate will gain hands-on exposure to AI Agents, automation, data analytics, MIS, finance processes, and SAP integration while working on real-world business use cases."

### Key Responsibilities
- Develop and implement **AI Agents for accounting and finance-related processes**
- Develop and automate **MIS, dashboards, and management reporting requirements**
- Explore and implement **AI-driven automation across business processes**
- Assist in **integrating AI-based solutions with the existing SAP environment**
- Identify **repetitive and manual processes** that can be improved through AI and automation
- Work with business teams to **understand requirements and translate them into technology-driven solutions**
- Support **data analysis / develop insights to improve business decision-making**
- Research **emerging Generative AI, Agentic AI, and automation technologies** and evaluate their business applications
- **Document processes, solutions, and automation workflows**

### Preferred Skills & Qualifications
- 🎓 **Currently pursuing a degree** in CS, IT, Data Science, AI/ML
- 🧠 **Strong interest in**: AI, Generative AI, Agentic AI, automation, data analytics
- 🐍 **Basic knowledge of Python** (preferred)
- 📊 Familiarity with data analysis, databases, APIs, or automation tools (advantage)
- 🏢 **Exposure to SAP, ERP systems, MIS, or finance processes is desirable** (not required)
- 🧠 Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities
- 🤝 Willingness to learn and work on real-world business problems

### What You Will Gain
- Hands-on experience in **implementing AI Agents in a real business environment**
- Exposure to **Finance, Accounting, MIS, SAP, and business automation**
- Experience collaborating with **cross-functional business teams**
- Understanding of **how AI can be integrated into enterprise processes**

### Stipend & PPO Opportunity
> "Selected students will receive a **stipend of ₹35,000 per month** during the internship. Based on individual performance, technical capabilities, learning ability, and future business requirements, deserving candidates may be considered for a **Pre-Placement Offer (PPO)**."

### Preferred Candidate Profile
> "We are particularly interested in students who demonstrate a strong interest in:
> - **Agentic AI** | **Generative AI** | **Data Analytics** | **Automation** | **MIS** | **SAP Integration** | **Finance Technology**"
