## 💡 Stand-Out Tips

1. ✅ **Build a "HackerRank-style" mini project** — e.g., a code execution sandbox, online judge, plagiarism checker, or assessment platform. This shows understanding of the domain.
2. ✅ **Get active on HackerRank's own platform** — solve problems, get a high rating, add badges to the HackerRank profile (link it in resume)
3. ✅ **Mention specific products** in the cover email: "I love how [Assessments / Interview / Chakra] works because..."
4. ✅ **Show DSA strength** — HackerRank values this (it's literally their business)
5. ✅ **Open-source contributions** = "may expedite the interview process" (HackerRank explicitly says this)
6. ✅ **Be specific about why HackerRank, not "any big company"** — they read 100s of generic applications
7. ✅ **Get referrals from current employees** — 4x higher response rate than cold applications
